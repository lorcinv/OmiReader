# OmiReader

A local library with folders, tags, bookmarks and recent reading. Tap the middle of a page to show the reading tools. Typography, page turns and full-screen preferences apply across books.

OmiReader uses four Omi palettes: Mist, Nord, Sand and Night. Book sources are optional and can be imported in Settings.

The reading and book-source engines are based on Legado, revision `da17bb2bed44f30b12a524c2457e32a20b16fa41`. Thanks to gedoor and all contributors. GPL-3.0 and third-party notices are included under Open-source licenses.
